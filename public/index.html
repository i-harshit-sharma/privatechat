PRIVACY

What the server holds, and for how long

The server is a relay. It passes things along and then forgets them.

A messageHeld until the other browser collects it, usually about a second. Deleted on pickup, or after 60 seconds if never collected.
A fileSame as a message: handed over once, then deleted. Gone after 60 seconds if never collected.
Your IDForgotten when you close the tab, or within 30 seconds if your connection drops.
Your nameKept in your own browser. Passed to the other person when a chat starts.
Chat historyNever stored. It exists only on the two screens and disappears with the tab.
AccountsThere are none. No email, no password, no profile.

One thing to know: messages travel over HTTPS but are not end-to-end encrypted. The relay could read a message during the moment it holds it, so do not treat PrivateChat as a tool for secrets that need stronger protection.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Opening the chat page gives you an ID. You type a display name, share the ID, and that is all.

What happens if I refresh or close the page?

Your chats end and your messages are gone. When you come back you get a new ID and start again.

Can I message someone who is offline?

No. Both people must have the page open. If an ID is not online, the request is refused.

Can I get an old conversation back?

No. Nothing is saved on the server or in your browser, so there is nothing to restore.

What if my connection drops for a moment?

You have about 30 seconds to come back before the chat ends. Messages sent to you in that time are delivered when you return.

Is it end-to-end encrypted?

No. Messages are encrypted in transit with HTTPS and deleted from the relay on delivery, but the relay is able to read them while it holds them.
